Overview

The Desert Tulip Hotel & Resort is an exquisite example of Rajasthani heritage and architecture. The magnificent palace rises majestically in the middle of the stunning Jaisalmer sand dunes, acting as an oasis of friendliness and hospitality. As soon as you step inside this prestigious royal mansion decorated with age-old style and elegance, you will feel the Rajwada khatirdaari. Walk like a royal through the arched hallways that lead to our 64 luxurious rooms and suites, each of which is furnished with tasteful interior design and modern conveniences. As you bask in the exotic view of the pool and beautiful grounds encircled by our gigantic mansion, feel the grandeur and grandiosity.

Dining

At Desert Tulip Hotel & Resort, dining is a celebration of flavors. Indulge in a symphony of culinary delights ranging from international cuisines to local specialties. Savor exquisite dishes meticulously crafted by seasoned chefs while enjoying the elegant ambiance. Each meal is a journey of taste and luxury.

Facilities

Swimming Pool

Swimming Pool

The swimming pool at Hotel Desert Tulip is a beautiful jewel that exudes tranquility. Due to its secluded location among the Thar Desert's golden dunes, the pool offers breathtaking views of the surrounding desert landscape. Whether you choose to cool down in the crystal-clear water or unwind on one of the luxurious sunbeds, the pool provides a welcome respite from the oppressive desert heat.

Gym & Fitness Center

Gym & Fitness Center

The gym and fitness center at the Hotel Desert Tulip is equipped with state-of-the-art training equipment, allowing guests to engage in a variety of workouts and routines. Whether a person prefers weight lifting, cardio exercise, or simply wants to stretch and unwind, the fitness center has something for them.

Experiences

Baithak

Our conference room is ideal for hosting an elegant corporate event or seminar because it is fully furnished with modern conveniences to guarantee a remarkable success.

Lehariya

You can have a luxurious experience in our courtyard. You can spend a quiet evening with your family and friends next to the bar and pool, and you'll carry with you memories of Rajasthani cultural programs, the ghoomar dance, and even DJs on demand.

Ciro

A restaurant serving multiple cuisines offers you wonderful treats made with regional ingredients.

Mehraab

A rooftop restaurant where you can enjoy delicacies from a broad variety of cuisines and mesmerizing views of the pool and nearby forts will provide you with an unforgettable dining experience.

Billiards

Discover a retreat for your mind, body, and soul with our exclusive and luxurious assortment of spa services. One can also locate a location to brighten their mood and demonstrate their cue stick technique by striking the pool balls.

Piscina

Discover tranquility when you dive into our pool and let the soothing water relax your body. Enjoy your favorite beverage or a few light appetizers by the stunning infinity pool.

Weddings & Events

Weddings

Jaisalmer's Hotel Desert Tulip weddings will live on in memory forever. For couples looking for a unique and amazing wedding experience, this superb hotel offers a mesmerizing setting in Rajasthan's enchanted Thar Desert.

The elegant design of Hotel Desert Tulip, which was inspired by traditional Rajasthani palaces, provides a regal setting for wedding celebrations. The luxurious banquet halls and expansive outdoor settings, which can accommodate both intimate parties and huge celebrations, allow for the perfect customization of any wedding.

Conference & Meetings

The Hotel Desert Tulip Jaisalmer is a premier venue for organizing conferences and meetings in the picturesque city of Jaisalmer, Rajasthan. This lavish hotel offers a seductive blend of modern comforts and old-world hospitality from Rajasthan, hidden away in the Thar Desert's golden dunes.

Due to its well-equipped conference facilities, the Hotel Desert Tulip Jaisalmer provides the ideal setting for business gatherings of various sizes. Large, flexible spaces at the hotel may accommodate anything from small-group CEO meetings to sizable conferences. Modern multimedia technology and speedy internet access ensure that the event will go smoothly and successfully for all attendees.
