Overview

Welcome to Narayan Niwas Palace, a magnificent 19th-century ancestral palace thoughtfully restored into a luxurious 4-star heritage hotel in the heart of Jaisalmer. Experience the timeless charm of Rajasthan with elegant architecture, modern comforts, warm hospitality, and an authentic royal atmosphere. Discover the beauty of Jaisalmer while enjoying a memorable stay where heritage, comfort, and tradition come together beautifully.

Why Choose Narayan Niwas Palace ?

  • Prime Location
  • Luxury Accommodations
  • Exquisite Dining

Dining

Experience exceptional dining at Narayan Niwas Palace, where authentic flavors, elegant ambiance, and warm hospitality come together to create unforgettable culinary moments. Inspired by the rich heritage of Rajasthan, our restaurant offers a perfect setting for memorable dining experiences in the heart of Jaisalmer.

Ambience and Seating

Designed with royal elegance and modern comfort, our dining space offers a welcoming atmosphere for families, couples, and groups. Whether you prefer intimate dining or celebrating special occasions, our beautifully designed seating creates the perfect experience.

Our Menu

Our carefully curated menu brings together authentic Rajasthani delicacies, traditional Indian flavors, and selected multi-cuisine specialties. Prepared using fresh ingredients and rich culinary traditions, every dish is crafted to deliver an exceptional dining experience.

Special Events and Private Dining

Experiences

Create unforgettable memories at Narayan Niwas Palace with unique experiences that showcase the rich culture, heritage, and beauty of Jaisalmer. From desert adventures to cultural evenings, every experience is designed to make your stay truly special.
