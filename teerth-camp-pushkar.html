Experiences

Camel Safari

Explore the desert landscape surrounding Pushkar on a traditional camel safari. Experienced guides lead guests through sandy dunes, providing insights into the local flora, fauna, and nomadic way of life.

Cultural Performances

Enjoy vibrant cultural performances showcasing Rajasthani music and dance forms such as folk dances like Ghoomar and Kalbelia. These performances often take place in the evening around a bonfire, creating a magical ambiance under the starlit sky.

Nature Walks

Embark on guided nature walks to explore the natural beauty and biodiversity of the Pushkar region. Traverse scenic trails, encounter indigenous flora and fauna, and learn about the unique ecosystems of the desert landscape.

Sightseeing Tours

Discover the cultural and historical treasures of Pushkar and its surroundings with guided sightseeing tours. Visit iconic landmarks such as the Brahma Temple, Pushkar Lake, Savitri Temple, and explore nearby forts, palaces, and heritage villages.

Yoga and Meditation

Start your day with yoga and meditation sessions amidst the serene desert landscape. Experienced instructors guide guests through rejuvenating yoga poses and meditation techniques, promoting relaxation, mindfulness, and inner peace.
