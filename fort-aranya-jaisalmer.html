Overview

The opulent building, perched atop a 15-foot-high platform, honors the Rajputs' former greatness and legacy while providing visitors with a stay in the height of luxury. It is the perfect place to escape from the rush of modern life and refresh your senses.

The resort's special amenities make it an ideal destination that leaves visitors with pleasant memories and a desire for more. They include an indoor 4x4 dirt track to test and improve driving skills, a firing/archery range, games zone, kite flying, obstacle course, Jeep and camel safari, star gazing, dune dinners, soundproof disco, conference hall, and more.

Rooms & Suites

The Royal Suites

The design and decoration of the Royal Suites is based on a theme. The Maharaja/Maharani suite offers all the pleasures, including an exclusive sitting area, a private viewing balcony, as well as a private plunge pool and deck, and transports guests back in time. Any of the suites will make you feel like absolute royalty during your stay.

The Royal Tents

It is the perfect option for a luxurious stay because of its distinctive design, which incorporates a variety of pleasures including a private sitting area, terraces, and plunge pools with a well-appointed bedroom and a number of other perks. There is air conditioning in the tents.

The Luxury Rooms

The Luxury rooms include a variety of amenities and are designed for comfort. In addition to the fundamental amenities, the tastefully decorated furnishings and lighting elevate these and make them a comfortable choice.

The Luxury Tents

It's a memorable spot to stay and enjoy the desert because of the distinctive design that incorporates a variety of comforts. There is air conditioning in the tents.

Dining

Fort Aranya in Jaisalmer offers an enchanting dining experience within ancient walls. Its panoramic views, traditional Rajasthani cuisine, and cultural ambiance immerse diners in history. The blend of flavors and historic charm captivates guests.

Weddings & Events

Fort Aranya, nestled in the majestic Jaisalmer, offers a regal backdrop for weddings & events. Its ancient charm, sprawling courtyards, and palatial architecture evoke a timeless allure, creating an unforgettable setting for celebrations steeped in history and elegance.
